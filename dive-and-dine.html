Dive & Dine

For dive resorts, liveaboards and yachts. Food made for the rhythm of days at sea.

Dive & Dine is a concept built around a simple truth: the best diving days deserve equally good food. Not resort buffets, not packaged meals. Real food, made with care, that understands where you just came from.

We work with dive resorts, liveaboard operators and private yachts to create food experiences that match the quality of the diving. Menus are built around the location, the season, and the people on board. Fresh, light, restorative where it needs to be — and worth celebrating when the dive was worth it.

Our list of partner locations and operators is curated and honest. No paid placements. Every recommendation comes from people who have been there, dived it, and eaten well — or not well enough — to know the difference.

What to expect
  • On-site cooking for dive resorts and liveaboards
  • Menus built around the location and season
  • Partner operator and restaurant recommendations across Indonesia
  • Available for day trips, multi-day charters and resort residencies
Who it is for

Dive operators, liveaboard owners, resort F&B managers, and private yacht guests who refuse to compromise on food after a great dive.
