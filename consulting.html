Culinary Consulting

Honest direction for restaurants, resorts, teams and new openings.

We work with restaurants, hotels and food businesses across Bali and Indonesia. Every engagement starts the same way: we listen. We want to understand the business, the team, the guest, and what is not working before we say anything about what should change.

Our approach is B2B consulting focused on consistent, steady growth. We are not here to redesign your brand or impose a concept. We are here to help you do what you already do — better, more consistently, and in a way that holds up over time.

If something is not right for us, we will tell you and advise you with an alternative that suits your idea better. We prefer fewer, longer engagements over short-term fixes.

What to expect
  • Menu development and concept refinement
  • Kitchen operations and team training
  • F&B strategy for hotels and resorts
  • New opening support and concept creation
  • Long-term retainer engagements available
Who it is for

Restaurant owners, hotel F&B directors, and investors in the food and hospitality space across Bali and Indonesia.
