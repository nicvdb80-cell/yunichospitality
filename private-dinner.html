Private Dinner Events

A rare wine. Truffles. Caviar. An occasion that deserves its own menu.

Some evenings have a reason behind them. A bottle you have been waiting for. An ingredient in season. A group of people who deserve something that goes beyond a restaurant booking. That is where we come in.

Our chef designs the menu around what you bring to the table — literally. Tell us about the wine, the occasion, the people. We build the food from there. Nothing is decided in advance. Everything begins with your evening.

Yuli hosts. The evening moves at the pace you set. We handle the rest — sourcing, preparation, service, timing — so that by the time you sit down, there is nothing left to think about except the food and the people across from you.

What to expect
  • Menu designed around your occasion, wine or chosen ingredient
  • On-site preparation and service by Yuli and our culinary team
  • Sourcing of premium ingredients — truffles, wagyu, caviar and more
  • Available for 4 to 30 guests in private settings
  • Bali and international dates on request
Who it is for

Anyone marking an occasion that deserves more than a restaurant. A birthday, an anniversary, a business dinner, or simply a reason to eat very well.
