Our Story

Born from a love of
Indonesian food

Yunic Hospitality started with a simple idea — that Indonesian cuisine is one of the most layered, diverse, and generous food cultures in the world, and not enough people outside of Indonesia really know it yet.

Founded by Yuli, who grew up surrounded by the food traditions of the archipelago, and led in the kitchen by Executive Chef Nic, whose cooking career has taken him across Europe and Asia. Yunic brings these two worlds together in a way that feels honest and personal.

We are based in Bali and work across private dining, culinary events, pop-ups, chef collaborations, and consulting. Everything we do is built around one question: how do we share Indonesian food culture in a way that genuinely connects with people?

Yuli & Nic Founder & Executive Chef, Yunic Hospitality · Bali

Culinary Consulting

04
Culinary Journeys

Join Nic on a small-group culinary journey through a specific city or province in Indonesia. Each trip explores local street food, markets, and restaurants together — eating, asking questions, and understanding the food from the inside. During the journey, Nic cooks one private dinner for the group using what was found along the way, and hosts one hands-on cooking class rooted in the flavours of that region.

Join a Journey
05
Bali Food Tours

Small-group food tours around Bali, led by Yuli and sometimes Nic. We visit the wet market and fish market in the early morning, walk through the stalls, talk about what we see, and end with a proper meal at a local warung. Other tours take you to farms we work with directly, finishing with babi guling or whatever is best that day. Or up to Kintamani to visit coffee farms and understand where the flavour actually starts. Real Bali, no tourist filter.

The People Behind Yunic

A founder who knows
the food. A chef who loves it.

Yunic Hospitality was built by two people with very different backgrounds and one shared obsession — Indonesian food and what it can mean for the people who taste it.

Yuli
Founder & Owner

Yuli grew up in Indonesia, surrounded by food that was cooked with care and eaten with family. She founded Yunic Hospitality to share that culture, not as a tourist version, but as it really is. She hosts every private dinner personally and makes sure every guest feels like they belong there.

Nic
Executive Chef & Culinary Director

Nic has cooked in kitchens across Europe and Asia. At Yunic, he works with Indonesian ingredients and flavour traditions, not to replicate traditional dishes, but to understand them deeply and cook from that place honestly. The result is food that tastes like Indonesia, with a technique that makes every element count.
